# Suggestions and learning

## What is suggested, and from what

Fill in the observed fields and a judgment field gets a suggested value when the values filled in settle it: the sidecar remembers the values people saved together (the Lowline engine, no model). Each value filled in lends strength to the values saved alongside it — or, for a field its replay rested on a few fields (below), each value in those fields — and values filled in that agree add up; a judgment field whose strongest value is strong enough — where replaying its saved documents shows that strength gets it right often enough — is suggested that value. The suggestion says which filled-in value it rests on most. A choice field is suggested only a choice it offers now: a value confirmed under a choice since removed from the template stays in its documents but is not suggested, and is again if the choice comes back. When the values filled in settle nothing, it suggests nothing, and says so.

A value seen with few saved documents lends less than one seen with many, and how much less is each field's own, chosen by replaying the saved documents: a field naming the very thing judged — the business an inspection is of, the aircraft an accident happened to — keeps the value it was first seen with, so a value of it seen once already speaks for the judgment; a broad field whose values go with many judgments lends little until it has been seen often. So a business inspected before is answered by its own record rather than by the kind of inspection. A request seen once, in a form where another field has decided the judgment all along, may settle nothing by itself.

A judgment field already confirmed in the document — typed in, or taken from its suggestion — counts as a value filled in for the others, so taking one suggestion can settle the next. A suggestion shown and not taken counts for nothing. A document does not record the order its values came in, so the replay takes its judgment fields as confirmed in the template's order; for one confirmed out of that order, the replay counts on a value its suggestion did not have.

A suggestion is drawn by its field: the value to take — shown in an empty text field in place of its placeholder — what it rests on, and a way to decline it. A suggestion is not a value until the person takes it; taking it and saving is a confirmation, and only confirmations are learned from. A value another program wrote into a document as a suggestion (`lowline.proposed`, see [Edits from outside](outside-edits.md)) is offered the same way, saying who wrote it, and is not learned from until a person decides it.

Where the values filled in settle nothing, a similar document may answer: the value confirmed in the saved document whose words read most like this one's — on a field where that has proven itself, and only there. Documents that read alike are often judged differently, and how often differs from field to field, so it is decided per field by a replay of its own (below): similar documents are suggested from only where they were right often enough over at least 30 of the replay's answers — on fewer, their share right swings too far to promise anything. Such a suggestion names the document it comes from. A field whose values are decided by what is written in it rather than by another field — an injury described in words, a request in its own phrasing — is thus suggested to once it has enough history, where values saved together would never settle it. Similar documents are not asked while the person types into the field. The documents most like a saved one are there to look at in any case, under "비슷한 사례" on its page, when the person opens it — each with the value it confirmed in each judgment field, as the vault holds it.

## How often is often enough

The strength a field's values saved together need is chosen per field by replaying its saved documents in the order they were saved: the lowest strength down to which the replayed suggestions were right 80% of the time, in every band and not only taken together — counted on every value people saved for the field, those taken from a suggestion or changed after one included, so a suggestion's misses count against it. It is chosen once a field has enough saved documents, and again as they grow by a tenth or as its choices change; until then nothing is suggested for the field. The chosen strengths are kept on this device beside the vault's cache, so a later launch uses them at once rather than replaying again — except the first launch after an update of what measures it (the engine), which replays once more, since an update may measure strength on another scale.

Before the strength, the same replay chooses which fields a judgment field rests on. Where a few fields decide a field with many values and the others only add a little to every value's strength, resting on those few is suggested more often at the same precision: the replay tries each field alone, adds them while each addition is suggested more often, and keeps that set only if it does better than every field together. Then only the values in those fields count for the field, and the learning view names them. Otherwise every value filled in counts. The fields are chosen again whenever the strength is.

A judgment field whose value is typed out — a text field with no choices — is asked again while the person types into it, after each pause: it stays open, and only a value that begins with what has been typed is suggested, at a strength chosen for that many characters. Korean begins to match while a syllable is still being composed: what is typed is compared key by key, so a value is offered before the syllable is finished. A person types where the suggestion before did not do, so the strengths for one, two and three characters are chosen by replaying each saved document as typed from its start, on the documents the strength without typing did not get right. The fields such a field rests on are chosen by its answers before and while typing together: the fields that decide it before anything is typed can blur it once a few characters have narrowed its values. A value suggested at fewer characters stays suggested while what is typed goes on to begin it — typing further narrows the values, it does not take back one already offered — even when the history is too short for a strength of its own at that length. A field whose values the filled-in ones narrow to a few but do not decide may get nothing before typing and the right value after a first character. While typing, a suggestion that only repeats what was typed is not drawn, and no reason is given for there being none.

The strength at which similar documents answer is chosen by the same replay, over the documents the values saved together left unsuggested — those are harder than the rest, and a strength chosen over all of them would promise more than it gives — and it is offered only when what it promises rests on at least 30 of its answers.

A judgment field whose template names its class (`lowline.coarse`) may be offered its class where its value is not: the first characters the values settled alongside one the document has share, held to the same target as a value on a replay of its own — over the documents whose value was not suggested. It is offered where a value would be, said to be only the start of the value; taken, it goes into the field with the cursor after it, and the field stays open for the rest, asked about as it is typed. A class is never offered while the field is being typed into.

A judgment field left without a suggestion says why beside it: nothing confirmed yet; its history has not yet shown that the values saved together are right often enough (with how many confirmed documents it had to learn from); the values filled in settle nothing; or suggestions are not available right now, while typing and saving go on. The sidecar that suggests is started again if it ends during a session — a few times at most — and is handed the vault again, so suggestions come back without the person doing anything.

## The record of what happened

When a document is saved, what happened to each suggestion — accepted, corrected or rejected — is appended to `.lowline/events/<device>.jsonl` in the vault, one file per install, with the template's version, the fields already filled when it was made (names only, in the order they were filled), how many characters had been typed in the field when the suggestion was first shown, and when it was shown and taken or rejected — with how many characters were in the field at that moment. A class taken counts as accepted when the value saved begins with it, and is recorded apart from the suggestions typing the rest brought (`source: coarse`). Suggestions read these files back: a field whose suggestion was last rejected in a document is not suggested in that document again, even after it is reopened.

An event records the template version it was made under; it counts for the template of that id as the template is now.

## The learning view

For each judgment field, the learning view shows how often recent suggestions were taken as offered — counted over the fields that got a suggestion, decision by decision, from the event files — so whether suggestions improve with use is something to look at, not to assume. Until ten have been decided it gives the count right, not a share. The decisions are also counted apart by where each suggestion came from — a value settled alongside one the document has, or a similar record. What people did with values another program proposed, and with classes offered where the value was not, is counted on lines of their own and not in the curve, which is how the app's own value suggestions fared.

Because that count leaves out the fields that got no suggestion, a field whose strength has been chosen also shows how its saved documents did on replay: how often one was suggested at all, and how often it was right. A replay's share right counts every document since the first, so a field that did better long ago keeps it high after it has changed, and the strength was chosen on those very answers, so a few of them flatter it. What to expect next is therefore said apart, as a floor: the share right among the latest 5,000 documents (all of them, for a form with fewer), lowered to what the suggestions it rests on can vouch for — "at least", with how many suggestions it was counted on. On many suggestions it hardly differs from the replay's share; on a few dozen it is several points below. When the replay rested the field on a few fields, it names them beneath. A field whose replay never reached the target shows how close it came: how often its answers were right at the most precise strength that still gathered enough of them. When that reaches the target taken together but a band within it falls short, it says so rather than calling the field short of the target. A field whose value is typed out also shows how typing did on its replay, whether or not it is suggested before typing: for each number of characters a strength was chosen for, how many documents were typed that far, how often one was suggested and how often it was right — or that no number of characters reached the target. A field with a class shows how the class did on its replay: of the documents whose value was not suggested, how often a class was, and how often it was right. Once a strength was chosen for similar records, a field shows how they did too — of the documents values saved together left unsuggested, how often a similar record answered and how often it was right — and whether they are suggested from yet, or how many answers their promise rests on short of the 30 it needs. A field suggested only from similar records says so in place of why values saved together are not. While a field's strengths are being chosen again — its saved documents grew or shrank by a tenth — what it shows is from before, and it says so until the new ones replace it.

Beneath the curves, weekly counts of those decisions and of the suggestions this device showed — by week, form and judgment field, by where each suggestion came from and by how many characters had been typed when each was shown (none, one, two, three or more), and within that by how long each decision took from the suggestion showing to its being taken or rejected (in spans that double from half a second to 32 seconds and over, with what was taken without further typing apart from what was taken after it), with forms and fields numbered and nothing else — can be copied to hand to someone studying the curves. Which number is which is shown beside them and never copied. Decisions on values another program proposed, and on classes, are counted with the rest and apart under their source. For each form they also give how many documents it holds with a judgment field filled by a person (a value only proposed does not count), how many of those the app has never saved (written by another program, so no one may have confirmed their values — the learning screen says so above the curves), and how many a week: a document counts in the week of the day its file is named by — the day it was made, or for a record brought in, its own day — so a history brought in all at once is not read as a busy week.
